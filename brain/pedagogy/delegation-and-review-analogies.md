**Rule.** The analogy register for **delegating work to AI and reviewing what comes back**, on the same terms as [[ai-analogies-chosen-and-rejected]] and [[our-analogies-chosen-and-rejected]]: one picture per concept, recorded once, never reinvented per lesson. Three registers exist because each hit the 400-word cap, not because the domains are unrelated — **check all three before inventing a picture.**

**Why separate.** The AI register answers "what is this machine and how do I work with it". These answer "what do I hand over, and how do I judge what returns". `ai-era-engineering-judgment` teaches both, so the pictures must not blur.

**The house choices, each with the alternative it beat.** Rejections are recorded here as the choice is made — the discipline the sibling register asked the next course to start.

- **Delegate by verifiability = a translator for a language you can read, versus one you cannot.** Same fluency either way; only your ability to check changes. Rejected: *handing work to an intern* — the AI register already spends the colleague image on four concepts, and a fifth use blurs delegation into agency.
- **Diff over explanation = the label on the tin versus what is in the tin.** Printed by whoever filled it, at the same moment; nobody opened the tin. Rejected: *walk the site, not the site report* — collides with renovating a building in the web register.
- **Tests written by the same tool = marking your own homework.** Good marks, nothing you did not already believe. Rejected: *an auditor auditing themselves* — a role, not a picture.
- **Scrutiny at the seams = leaks happen at the joints, not the middle of the pipe.** Nobody torches a straight run; they check every fitting, because pressure finds a way out there. Rejected: *a garment's seams* — no pressure, so a weaker boundary.
- **When to stop delegating = a taxi stuck in traffic; at some point you walk.** The fare on the meter is not a reason to stay in the car. Rejected: *sunk cost* — a term, not an image.

**All five survive Japanese** ([[every-analogy-must-survive-japanese]]): each is a plain physical scene with no English wordplay — 翻訳者, 缶のラベル, 自分の答案を自分で採点する, 配管の継ぎ目, 渋滞のタクシー.

**The misconception this pre-empts.** That a register is bookkeeping. It is the reason a learner who meets the same concept in two courses builds one model of it rather than two.
